A question every person has asked since being born, and will keep asking until the end of their life, is: who am I?
We humans constantly try to define everything around us, and we always end up back at the starting point. Like everyone, I too have asked myself this question, looking for an answer that would satisfy me as much as possible. Alas, and this is a secret, I haven't found it. So what do I do?

In engineering, a pattern that comes up very often when solving a problem is to break it down, but also to make it as easy as possible and see whether it works: to see whether the model we develop is able to solve the problem. A very practical example is the law of gravity. We know that on Earth the gravitational acceleration is 9.81 m/s^2. It isn't the same at every point on Earth, but it works, and it's good enough.

In my opinion we should approach the heaviest, hardest and even metaphysical questions of life with this method: find, for the moment, the answer to that question that satisfies you enough for now. It doesn't have to be a final solution, but for the moment it has to work for you. You feel this not from a rational point of view but purely by instinct: this is true, or it isn't.

You can invent an answer yourself, which is usually the harder but, in the long run, preferable path, but you can also start with a definition or a way of thinking that feels right to you. I found mine in a very old cartoon, Ghost in the Shell, a masterpiece of 2D Japanese animation.

There are countless elements that form the body and mind of human beings, just as there are countless components that make me an individual with my own personality. Of course I have a face and a voice that distinguish me from everyone else, but my thoughts and my memories belong only to me, and I am aware of my destiny. Each of these things is nothing more than a small part of the whole. I gather data in my own way and use it in my own way, and this creates a mixture that gives me shape as an individual and from which my consciousness emerges. I feel imprisoned, free to expand only within predetermined boundaries.

This quote gives a good context for this "philosophy", if we want to call it that: in the end **I believe we are the sum of our actions and experiences**. Every little thing we do is part of our life, however small. I would also add that the more **emotional weight** a situation carries, **the more that moment becomes part of our life and experience**. That is why we remember our first kiss much better than the 10 minutes spent queuing at the post office or somewhere else.

A trivial observation that we usually forget in our daily life. But this knowledge, if we want to call it that, is useful for choosing and taking actions! That is, I could choose to do or take part in one event rather than another by asking myself: "Which of these two possibilities will bring me more **positive and healthy** emotions in the long term?" This is a very simple and fast method for choosing the right path. I deliberately included *positive and healthy*, because otherwise I could say I'll go clubbing every night and party in glory (at least for me that isn't it, XD). The nice thing is that **positive and healthy** is not an absolute definition; it is relative to us and our actions. Some prefer to go running in the evening, climb until midnight, stay home playing on the computer, call a loved one on the other side of the world, it doesn't matter. You have to do what you think is right for you! And nobody has an answer for you.

But it is the ability to choose, whatever you think is true, that makes us human. There is no book or teacher to give you the answer or to show you the path. Choose your own way. Do not follow me or anyone else.

By following this reasoning, or way of thinking, you start thinking for yourself (at least that's how it was for me). As a child you don't know how the world works, so you rely on the people around you: Mum, Dad, friends, etc. Maybe that works for you, but at some point you realise, or feel, that there are many things that "don't suit you", as happens to a lot of people. Then you decide to change and do things your own way. This may mean going against the expectations of many people, but that's fine!
