This is the beginning of a new chapter, and of new content entering the world. I don't know how or what will happen on this page, but for now I'm glad to have a tool where I can write and share my ideas, and not only speak them out loud.
To anyone reading this post: thank you for being here. Whatever brought you to this corner of the internet, you are welcome. Now, let me figure out how to put an image in here.

In the future I intend to write posts and articles in English too, but for now I feel more at ease in Italian. So what can you expect to read on this blog? Some of my personal ideas, some reflections, and a few of the eureka moments I've had in my life. In short, topics I'd like to discuss with anyone who wants a deep conversation like I do, but for some reason haven't yet had the chance to, either for lack of time or because I haven't met you in person yet, dear reader.

So for a first article I should write the one about identity, and after that one about neural networks, or about what I think of what I do. Even better, I should also add a page for all the kinds of content and little projects I've made.
